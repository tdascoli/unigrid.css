General Information

Yellowstone National Park covers 2,219,791 acres across three states. The park has five entrances and is open year-round, though some roads and facilities are seasonal.

Fees & Passes

Vehicle: $35 (7 days). Individual: $20. Annual Pass: $70. Lifetime Senior Pass: $80.

Hours of Operation

The park is open 24 hours a day, year-round. Visitor centers typically open 8am–6pm in summer, reduced hours in winter.

Contact

General Info: (307) 344-7381. Emergency: 911. Website: nps.gov/yell
